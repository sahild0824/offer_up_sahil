## 7. 2026 rule changes affecting fantasy

Sources: https://www.steelers.com/news/nfl-owners-approve-2026-nfl-rule-changes , https://www.vikings.com/news/nfl-rule-change-bylaws-annual-league-meetings-2026-season , https://www.cbssports.com/nfl/news/nfl-rule-changes-2026 , https://www.4for4.com/2026/preseason/nfl-rule-changes-and-how-they-impact-2026 , https://www.footballzebras.com/2026/04/nfl-owners-approve-rule-changes-for-the-2026-season/

Five approved playing-rule changes (owners meeting, spring 2026):
1. Onside kicks may be declared/attempted at ANY point in the game (previously 4th quarter only). Fantasy: marginally more possessions for trailing teams and more garbage-time/comeback drives; slight DST/kicker variance.
2. Out-of-bounds kickoff from the 50 (after certain penalties) now spots the ball at the 20 instead of the 25 — removes incentive to kick OOB.
3. Kickoff setup-zone alignment: only 5 receiving-team players must be on the restraining line (was 6); 4 may be off it (was 3). Special-teams only.
4. Replay/league officiating may now consult on-field officials to eject for flagrant football or non-football acts even if no flag was thrown. Fantasy: small availability risk for hot-headed players.
5. One-year provision letting the NFL Officiating department correct "clear and obvious" misses — contingency for replacement officials during a possible officials' work stoppage. **Note: this implies an officials' labor dispute is possible; not a scoring change.**
No changes to scoring plays, PAT, overtime, or the "tush push" were found. Net fantasy impact: minimal; no direct scoring/usage effect on skill players.
