# Round-by-round debates: what the forums and experts say (Sept 5, 2026)

Four parallel researchers swept the market for every player on every round's target list in the
10-team, full-PPR, pick-4 plan, then compared the consensus against our model and our plan. The raw
reports are `rounds_1_2.md`, `rounds_3_4.md`, `rounds_5_8.md` and `rounds_9_14.md`; every claim in
them carries the URL of the snippet it came from. The per-round summaries are rendered in the app's
Plan tab under "What the forums and experts say".

## Sourcing limits (important)

About 170 searches were run across the four sweeps. **reddit.com returned zero results in every one
of the ~35 Reddit-targeted queries** (`site:reddit.com`, `r/fantasyfootball`, thread-title phrasings),
and direct fetches of reddit.com and old.reddit.com are blocked by this environment's egress proxy.
Every fetch to a fantasy outlet (ESPN, CBS, Yahoo, PFF, SI, FantasyPros, PFN, FTN, 4for4, Fantasy
Life, Footballguys, Draft Sharks, RotoBaller, NBC, NFL.com) was blocked too, so **all evidence is
search-result snippet text**, quoted verbatim and attributed. The single forum source anyone
retrieved is the Footballguys "Picks 4-6 Discussion" thread. Nothing here is invented: where a
sweep found no forum signal, its report says so rather than substituting one.

## Where the model and the market disagree

Kept deliberately, with the disagreement surfaced in the app:

- **George Pickens** — the most-cited regression call in the round-3 range (FantasySP, DK Network),
  but our composite is 23 with bust 21. Kept.
- **Stefon Diggs** — NFL.com's tenth-round sleeper and a "strong WR3 with upside" per FantasyPros;
  our model has him at boom 20 / bust 98 on the age-33 decline. Listed as a floor play, not upside.
- **George Kittle** — the market's "discounted upside" tight end; our model scores him bust 99 and
  risk 99. Listed as a swing.
- **Malik Nabers** — the market still drafts him in the third; our model has bust 95 and risk 100,
  which is why he moved behind London and Collins rather than off the board.

## Bench construction with five slots

The consensus for a 10-team, one-QB league: three or four running backs (one standalone-value
handcuff, the rest contingent), one or two rookie receiver darts, and **no QB2 or TE2** — FantasyPros
says a surplus at quarterback or tight end "wastes picks better spent elsewhere" because both are
replaceable off waivers. Handcuffing your own back is split: Draft Sharks calls it "the single most
underrated move in fantasy football" (starters miss time at roughly a 30% rate over 17 games), while
FTN's line is that "handcuffing isn't bad strategy, but automatically handcuffing is bad strategy".
