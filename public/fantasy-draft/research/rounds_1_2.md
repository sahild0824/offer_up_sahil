### Verdict vs our plan
- **Agree** with Gibbs > Bijan > Chase as the first three names: every source treats the trio (plus Nacua) as the non-negotiable top tier, and CBS/FTN/Yahoo lean Gibbs at 1.01, Field Yates leans Bijan. Either RB at 4 would be a gift.
- **Agree** on Nacua as the 4th name and on making him conditional. The industry default at 1.04 in PPR is Nacua (4for4, PFF, Fantasy Life headline), but the Sept 1 Deseret piece confirms the NFL review has no Week 1 deadline, SI calls his status "shaky", and he was side-working on Aug 31. Given a Thursday Sept 10 opener in Melbourne, the plan's "cleared before the opener" gate is the right gate. Note the Stafford back situation is an extra variable the forum crowd raises; we could not confirm his September status.
- **Agree** on JSN as the fallback; the only industry pushback is FTN's "slight fade" on the coordinator change. Amon-Ra St. Brown is the name 4for4 and the Footballguys thread add at this slot that we do not list (see "Missing names").
- **Agree** on Taylor as the RB alternative only (nobody puts him at 4 in PPR; ADP ~1.07).
- **Agree** on never McCaffrey at 4 in PPR: even his defenders (PFN) want him at 7-8; SI lists him as a bust; 4for4 only endorses him at 4 in standard.
