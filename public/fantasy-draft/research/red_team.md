# Red-team review of the 2026 draft model and plan (10-team, full PPR, pick 4)

Review date: September 5, 2026. Model frozen September 4. Injury model frozen August 9.

Evidence note. Fantasy/sports sites and reddit.com were blocked for direct fetch (PFF, ESPN and FFC fetches all returned EGRESS_BLOCKED). Every claim below comes from web-search snippets of the cited page, so treat all of it as secondhand unless it is a direct quote attributed to a named person in the snippet. No Reddit thread surfaced as a snippet despite four attempts; "community" evidence below is from mainstream outlets, not r/fantasyfootball. Where two sources conflict I say so.

## (A) The 15 most important problems, ranked

### 1. The plan's headline pick (Nacua at 4) ignores the model's own risk score, and the injury model never saw the psoas injury
- Evidence: Nacua has a psoas injury that "has lingered since the middle of August"; he returned to practice Aug 30 and McVay said Sept 4 he is "making good progress" (https://heavy.com/sports/nfl/los-angeles-rams/rams-puka-nacua-injury-update-australia-opener/, https://www.turfshowtimes.com/los-angeles-rams-news/147585/rams-nacua-garrett-donald-49ers-mcvay). The Rams open Thursday Sept 10 in Melbourne. Schefter said a Week 1 suspension "can not be ruled out" (https://www.nbcsportsbayarea.com/nfl/san-francisco-49ers/puka-nacua-suspension-adam-schefter/1955882/). He avoided the exempt list at cutdown; civil trial set for March 27, 2028; no NFL decision (https://www.deseret.com/sports/2026/09/01/puka-nacua-week-one-status-los-angeles-rams/, https://www.si.com/nfl/rams/onsi/puka-nacua-alaric-jackson-suspension-decision). SI's fantasy desk: "Nacua Juggles Injury, Fear of Suspension" and suggests focusing elsewhere early if you have not drafted (https://www.si.com/onsi/fantasy/injuries/rams-injury-suspension-training-camp-puka-nacua-fantasy-football, https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/potential-puka-nacua-suspension-fantasy-235718856.html).
- Counter-evidence (concede): PFF's 10-team pick-4 plan still makes Nacua the round-1 target (https://www.pff.com/news/fantasy-football-2026-perfect-draft-strategy-round-by-round-10-team-leagues-pick-4), and his ADP is still ~4.
- Why it matters at pick 4: the model gives Nacua risk 97 and bust 72, then the plan rule says "take Nacua unless Gibbs, Bijan or Chase falls." The plan overrides the model's own signal without saying why. The injury component of risk was frozen Aug 9, before the psoas issue existed, so 97 is not even the full picture.
- Fix: make the 1.04 rule conditional and dated. If the NFL announces discipline or Nacua is listed on the Week 1 report before draft night, drop him behind JSN and St. Brown (bust 1, risk 28). If he plays a full Thursday game with no discipline, keep him. Re-run the injury model with Sept 4 practice statuses (see #11). Expose the risk-adjusted EV at 1.04 in the Plan tab rather than a flat rule.

### 2. The round-2 "bell-cow at 17" list is built on 12-team ADP and on the model's own high-bust players
- Evidence: Achane's model ADP is 11.9 and Henry's 16.5; Walker is "the RB6 off the board and a borderline first-round pick on Sleeper" (https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/kenneth-walker-iii-fantasy-outlook-121917961.html). PFF's 10-team pick-4 plan treats Walker as a round-2 hope, not a lock (https://www.pff.com/news/fantasy-football-2026-perfect-draft-strategy-round-by-round-10-team-leagues-pick-4). Greedy pickers "pick the position which adds most value right now, not taking into account consequences for future picks"; MCTS "outperforms the greedy strategy" and greedy "seems to be worse than random" in one test (https://towardsdatascience.com/performance-of-monte-carlo-tree-search-for-fantasy-football-drafts-269075d08088).
- Internal contradiction: of the six named "bell-cows," the model's bust scores are Achane 88, Henry 89, Walker 83, Hampton 77, Cook 42, Chase Brown 4. Four of six are in the model's own top-quartile bust range.
- Why it matters: pick 17 is 2.07 in a 10-team room. Achane will not be there. If the runner counts him as a 17 target, the whole round-2 branch is mis-simulated and the fallback ("do not reach, pivot to Bowers/McBride/London/Collins") triggers more often than the plan expects.
- Fix: convert every ADP feed to a 10-team pick equivalent using rank, not raw pick number (FFC publishes a 10-team PPR ADP: https://fantasyfootballcalculator.com/adp/ppr/10-team/all; FantasyPros has a 10-team PPR draft-wizard ADP: https://draftwizard.fantasypros.com/football/adp/mock-drafts/overall/default-ppr-10-teams). Replace the greedy runner with a Monte Carlo over ADP stdev (FFC gives stdev) and report availability probability at 17 per player. Require the "17 list" to satisfy bust <= 60 or explain the exception.

### 3. 12-team ADP mis-prices QB and TE for a 10-team room, so the "Value" flag on Bowers and the QB window are format artifacts
- Evidence: in 10-team leagues "you might grab a QB or TE earlier than you typically would in a 12-team draft" (https://www.draftsharks.com/article/fantasy-football-draft-strategy-guide/10-team-ppr). PFF: "late in the second round is when you would expect the top quarterback, Josh Allen, and the top two tight ends, Trey McBride and Brock Bowers, to go off the board" in 10-team (https://www.pff.com/news/2026-fantasy-football-perfect-draft-strategy-round-by-round-for-10-team-leagues-picks-8-10). CBS's 10-team mock had Bowers in round 2 and Allen in round 4, with the explicit note that "in a shallow league where every manager has an all-star roster, you want to find a way to stand out" (https://www.cbssports.com/fantasy/football/news/fantasy-football-10-team-mock-ppr/). Yahoo drafters take QB 9.3 spots and TE 9.2 spots ahead of expert ranks (https://sports.yahoo.com/fantasy/article/2026-fantasy-football-how-to-build-the-best-team-possible-on-yahoo-using-adp-vs-xrank-to-identify-positional-value-190143205.html).
- Why it matters: Bowers's "Value" flag (composite 20.4 vs ADP 28.1) likely disappears in a 10-team room; the "pivot to Bowers/McBride at 17" branch may be fine, but the "Loveland in round 4" and "QB at 57-64" branches assume 12-team drift.
- Fix: use format-specific ADP (above) and show pick-availability probabilities for Allen, Bowers, McBride, Loveland, Daniels, Maye, Burrow, Hurts at 17/24/37/44/57/64.

### 4. The roster structure (WR, RB, then WR in rounds 3-6) walks into the RB dead zone with one RB, against 2026 consensus and against what 2025 showed
- Evidence: FantasyPros' 2026 RB strategy: "taking two RBs in the first two rounds is appealing (super-hero strategy), especially considering the value pocket at WR in Rounds 3-6, with a preference being two RBs by Round 3, with a minimum of one" (https://www.fantasypros.com/2026/08/fantasy-football-draft-strategy-targets-avoids-running-backs-2026/). PFF 10-team pick 4: "it generally makes sense to draft two running backs early, but 10-team leagues make it easier to add a third." FTN's in-season 2025 verdict: "The Zero/Hero RB contingent has to be in shambles in 2025, because the strategy has not paid off this season, and it's not like last year was any better" (https://ftnfantasy.com/nfl/the-death-of-zero-rb). Counter: DraftSharks' 10-team PPR guide argues the optimal 10-team start is Zero RB with four WRs plus elite TE/QB (https://www.draftsharks.com/article/fantasy-football-draft-strategy-guide/10-team-ppr). So there is genuine disagreement; the plan should not present Hero RB as settled.
- Why it matters: at 24 and 37 the plan is "hammer WR"; the model's own RB list shows Hampton 20.0, Walker 20.2, Jeanty 19.6, Henry 16.5, Love 27.7, Hall 33.3, Kyren 31.2 all in that window, several of which it avoids.
- Fix: add an RB-RB branch (17 and 24) when two of {Walker, Hampton, Brown, Cook, Henry} are projected available, and score both structures with the Monte Carlo rather than asserting one.

### 5. Bijan's risk 90 looks stale (hold-in ended a month ago) and contradicts his record
- Evidence: Bijan signed a three-year extension worth up to $75M in early August that "ended the running back's weeklong hold-in" (https://sports.yahoo.com/nfl/breaking-news/article/bijan-robinson-agrees-to-record-breaking-extension-worth-up-to-75-million-with-falcons-120201722.html, https://www.nfl.com/news/falcons-bijan-robinson-contract-extension). He "has yet to miss a game in his NFL career" (https://fantasyfootballcalculator.com/players/bijan-robinson/injury).
- Why it matters: if he falls to 4 the plan takes him, which is right, but a risk 90 might push a user toward Chase or Nacua over him. It also shows the ADP-trend-as-news signal and the expert-disagreement signal can carry August noise into September.
- Fix: decay ADP-trend and expert-spread inputs with a two-week half-life; zero contract-related risk once a deal is signed; add a "games missed, last 3 years" floor term.

### 6. Trey McBride situation 3 is a percentile artifact and the plan contradicts it
- Evidence: real headwinds exist (Brissett starting, new play-caller Mike LaFleur, Love drafted No. 3, TD regression), but consensus still has him "TE2 and 20th overall ... a strong draft target around the 20th pick" (https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/notes/419122/trey-mcbride-2026-outlook.php, https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/updated-trey-mcbride-fantasy-outlook-102919804.html).
- Internal contradiction: the plan's round-2 pivot names "Bowers / McBride." A player with the 3rd-worst situation at his position should not be a pivot target unless the situation score is not trusted.
- Why it matters: within-position percentiles on a pool of ~15-20 TEs turn a moderate concern into an extreme score. Same mechanism gives Kittle 99/99 and Kyler 98/98.
- Fix: for QB and TE, shrink each percentile toward 50 by (1 - n/40) or compute percentiles on the overall top-150 pool; show the raw z-score alongside.

### 7. Coaching-change handling appears to be sign-blind: Irving (situation 5) and JSN (situation 30) are penalized for changes the beat treats as neutral-to-positive
- Evidence: Bucs' new OC Zac Robinson "could be a difference maker for Irving" and Irving is "making a big impression" on him (https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/bucs-best-bucky-irving-2026-224836725.html, https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/updated-bucky-irving-fantasy-outlook-153535950.html); the real Irving concerns are shoulder surgery and Gainwell/Tucker touches (https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/notes/419046/bucky-irving-2026-outlook.php). Seattle promoted Brian Fleury, whose stated goal is "to maintain" Kubiak's offense, and Darnold is under contract through 2027 (https://www.nfl.com/news/new-seahawks-oc-brian-fleury-goal-is-to-maintain-klint-kubiak-s-offense, https://fulltimefantasy.com/2026/08/13/seattle-seahawks-fantasy-football-outlook-2026/). ETR argues OC changes are "underpriced edges" precisely because direction matters (https://establishtherun.com/fantasy-impact-of-nfl-coaching-changes-3/).
- Why it matters: JSN is the most likely alternative at 4 if Nacua is compromised; a situation 30 could wrongly demote him below St. Brown or McCaffrey. Irving lands on the avoid list at least partly for a change the beat reads as favorable.
- Fix: replace the binary "new play-caller" penalty with a signed feature (internal promotion = 0, scheme-continuity hire = 0, RB-friendly hire = +), and a separate QB-change feature.

### 8. Boom scores driven by article citations are a popularity index: Concepcion 100, Tate 99, Love 99, Kraft 99, Lemon 98
- Evidence: Concepcion is going at ADP ~120 as WR50 with "uncertain quarterback situation" (https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/updated-kc-concepcion-fantasy-outlook-122818323.html); the Browns' starter is Deshaun Watson and Dillon Gabriel is on IR (https://www.espn.com/nfl/story/_/id/49450773/cleveland-browns-2026-week-1-starting-quarterback-deshaun-watson-shedeur-sanders-dillon-gabriel-taylen-green). Kraft "will likely be on some sort of snap count in Week 1" (https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/green-bay-packers-news-latest-142743990.html). Love is 50-50 for Week 1 with a high ankle sprain (https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/cardinals-50-50-jeremiyah-love-132337459.html). The wisdom-of-crowds literature says the failure mode is exactly "media hype" (http://www.advancedfootballanalytics.com/2008/08/fantasy-football-and-wisdom-of-crowds.html).
- Why it matters: the plan tells the user to spend 104-137 on "rookies and handcuffs"; a citation-driven boom list will steer those picks to the most-written-about names rather than the best paths to volume.
- Fix: log-transform citation counts, normalize by ADP slot (citations per expected draft position), cap the citation component at 20% of boom, and up-weight the game-log weekly boom rate, which is the only part of boom with a backtest.

### 9. Plan lists contradict the scores in four places with no stated override
- Tate: boom 99 (rookie WR, the backtest's own boom factor) but on the avoid list. He is the Titans' presumed WR1 with a projected 18-22% target share at ADP ~60-71 (https://www.profootballnetwork.com/fantasy-football/carnell-tate-fantasy-outlook-updated-2026/, https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/adp/carnell-tate.php).
- Diggs: bust 97 but a plan sleeper. Beat view: clear WR2 in Washington with Daniels, WR35 rank vs WR40 ADP, ADP ~91 (https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/updated-stefon-diggs-fantasy-outlook-113912676.html). Either the bust score or the sleeper tag is wrong.
- Watson: bust 98 (and the model's "PUP vs active" question) but he is on the initial 53, atop the depth chart, with a four-year $110.5M extension from June (https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/packers-christian-watson-gets-frustrating-164814156.html). Note the 2025 "activated from PUP" and "$13.25M extension" items in search results are last year's news, not 2026.
- Evans: bust 100 on the 49ers at ADP ~54 while Pearsall is out for the season, Deebo was just signed, and Kittle is uncertain (https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/notes/419960/mike-evans-2026-outlook.php, https://www.kxan.com/sports-general/fantasy-football-2026-guide-complete-rankings-injuries-possible-suspensions-and-more/). Age-33 risk is real, but 100 says "worst bust profile at WR," which the target picture does not support.
- Fix: enforce a consistency rule (avoid requires bust >= 70 or risk >= 85 plus negative ADP value; sleeper requires boom >= 60 or situation >= 60) and print the override reason on the Plan tab when a human judgment breaks the rule.

### 10. The injury model (frozen Aug 9) is blind to almost every injury that matters this week
- Post-Aug 9 events the model cannot have: Nacua psoas (mid-Aug), Love high ankle sprain (Aug 17 vs Raiders; https://www.nfl.com/news/report-cardinals-rb-jeremiyah-love-unlikely-to-play-again-in-preseason-due-to-high-ankle-sprain), Jeanty ankle (late Aug; https://www.nfl.com/news/raiders-rb-ashton-jeanty-apparent-right-leg-injury), Chase hyperextended knee, limited this week (https://www.si.com/nfl/bengals/onsi/zac-taylor-updates-ja-marr-chase-s-injury-status-as-week-1-preparation-begins-01m1c7bk29sh), Pacheco MCL plus back, Week 1 "up in the air" (https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/notes/418458/jahmyr-gibbs-2026-outlook.php), Pearsall out for season, Jayden Higgins torn ACL, Jordyn Tyson IR, Dillon Gabriel IR (https://www.kxan.com/sports-general/fantasy-football-2026-guide-complete-rankings-injuries-possible-suspensions-and-more/).
- Why it matters: Jeanty risk 99 and Love risk 98 happen to be right, but for the wrong reasons (age/expert spread), and Chase risk 68 / Nacua risk 97 do not reflect current practice status.
- Fix: re-run the injury model with Sept 4 inputs, or add a manual practice-status layer (DNP/limited/full, PUP/IR/exempt) that overrides the calibrated probability for Week 1-4.

### 11. The composite double-counts the crowd and ignores accuracy
- Evidence: ECR is "one combined ranking built from the opinions of multiple fantasy football analysts" from "100+ experts" (https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/rankings/, https://www.footballnationusa.com/post/ecr-fantasy-football-expert-consensus-rankings). Many of the 19 outlets are themselves ECR contributors, so double-weighting ECR counts them twice. FantasyPros' own accuracy contest identifies rankers who beat consensus three years running (https://ftnfantasy.com/fantasy/nfl/rankings, https://www.fantasypros.com/2026/08/fantasy-football-draft-rankings-from-the-most-accurate-experts-nfl-preseason/). The wisdom-of-crowds condition is independence; it fails "when one or a few very influential experts shape opinions" (http://www.advancedfootballanalytics.com/2008/08/fantasy-football-and-wisdom-of-crowds.html).
- Why it matters: the composite is the primary board. Herding makes the "expert disagreement" bust feature artificially small for hyped players (Nacua, Walker) and large for contrarian cases (Diggs, Evans).
- Fix: drop ECR to 1x, weight outlets by trailing three-year accuracy, and add market ADP as a separate signal instead of only using it for availability.

### 12. Schedule strength and Vegas adjustments are treated as signal; the sources that publish them say they are not actionable
- Evidence: Fantasy Index, "Beware of schedule difficulty": schedule release is "mostly useless from a fantasy perspective because FPA data can change so drastically from one season to the next" (https://fantasyindex.com/2026/07/21/scheduletron/beware-of-schedule-difficulty). Sharp Football: Vegas-based SOS metrics are "superior to other approaches, though even the best ones are not actionable for fantasy drafts" (https://www.sharpfootballanalysis.com/fantasy/strength-of-schedule-fantasy-football/).
- Why it matters: playoff-week weighting is a small-sample bet on defenses that do not exist yet in September; it can flip situation scores near the margin (e.g., McMillan 12, London 17).
- Fix: cap schedule at 5% of situation, drop playoff-week weighting until Week 8, keep Vegas implied team totals as the only schedule-like input.

### 13. Jonathan Taylor boom 22 and Etienne boom 3 look like the boom metric penalizes rushing-heavy profiles rather than measuring ceiling
- Evidence: Taylor is coming off a 20-TD season with a career-high 55 targets; consensus projects "top-four running back" with "legendary upside" (https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/jonathan-taylor-fantasy-outlook-2026-120518237.html, https://www.profootballnetwork.com/fantasy-football/jonathan-taylor-fantasy-outlook-updated-2026/). Etienne is in a full-time role in New Orleans with Kamara "already expected to miss time," ranked RB17 by Mike Clay, projected 1,070 rushing yards (https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/updated-travis-etienne-fantasy-outlook-142208241.html, https://www.espn.com/fantasy/football/story/_/id/47513496/2026-fantasy-football-rankings-ppr-mike-clay).
- Why it matters: Taylor is a live alternative at 4 if Nacua is compromised; boom 22 would talk the user out of a player whose ceiling was demonstrated last year. Etienne is on the avoid list with a 3.
- Fix: audit the weekly boom-rate definition; if it is a PPR-points threshold it should not be position-percentiled against pass-catching RBs only. Report the 2025 weekly top-12 rate directly.

### 14. Late-QB is right in general but the 10-team QB window (57-64) is too specific and may be empty
- Evidence for late QB: Allen was 2025 QB1 at 21.4 PPG, "the lowest average for a QB1 overall since 2009," and "outscored Drake Maye by less than one point per game"; Lamar finished QB20 (https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/fantasy-football-quarterback-draft-strategy-121824571.html). FantasyPros' Sept piece says late-round QB "is back in a big way" with ~13 confident starters (https://www.fantasypros.com/2026/09/fantasy-football-4-late-round-quarterbacks-to-draft-2026/). Evidence against a fixed window: in 10-team rooms drafters go earlier on QB (Allen in R4 in the CBS mock; Yahoo QB drafted 9.3 spots early).
- Why it matters: if Daniels/Maye/Burrow/Hurts go in rounds 4-5 in this room, the plan's next stop is 97+, which is fine only if the user trusts Stafford/Kyler/Baker. Kyler is boom 98/situation 98 in the model and a consensus sleeper in Minnesota (https://sports.yahoo.com/articles/updated-kyler-murray-fantasy-outlook-165721241.html), so the fallback is defensible, but it should be a tier trigger, not a pick number.
- Fix: replace "57-64" with "when the QB4-QB9 tier has one player left," computed from 10-team ADP; keep 97+ as the default.

### 15. "ADP trend as a news proxy" lags a week behind real news (exempt list, IR, trades)
- Evidence: Jacobs went on the exempt list Aug 30-31 and Green Bay traded for Kaleb Johnson (https://www.nfl.com/news/nfl-places-packers-rb-josh-jacobs-commissioner-exempt-list, https://www.si.com/onsi/fantasy/news/fantasy-impact-packers-acquire-kaleb-johnson-nfl-places-josh-jacobs-on-exempt-list). ESPN/Yahoo ADP are trailing averages, so the Sept 4 blend still contains pre-exempt drafts. Same lag applies to Pearsall, Higgins, Tyson, Gabriel.
- Why it matters: any player whose ADP is still "normal" because of trailing averages will look like value.
- Fix: hard-code status overrides (exempt, IR, PUP, season-ending) to ADP = undrafted; use only the last 7 days of FFC/Sleeper for the trend feature.

Honorable mentions (lower stakes): the ffanalytics package defaults to its own scoring and must be set to full PPR or RB/WR ordering drifts; a Bayesian floor/ceiling pulled from one GitHub repo has no independent validation; "Kicker and defense in the last two rounds" is fine but a 10-team room will have K/DST going a round earlier than 12-team ADP suggests.
