### 1. Herbert @BUF vs Bryce Young @CLE
- **What Young did in Wk2:** 23/36, 287 yds, 3 TD, 0 INT, 116.3 rating, 24.08 fantasy pts in a 34-3 road blowout at ATL [S13][S15]. He is the overall QB2 (behind Allen) at 27.8 ppg with 3 TD passes in each game [S15][S4]. He practiced fully Wed [S16].
- **Herbert:** 401 yds, 2 TD, 3 INT in two games, hasn't reached 15 pts [S5][S3], 29th of 33 in ANY/A [S4], now without both TEs and his starting LG [S7][S9], on a cross-country trip as a 7-point dog [S40][S4].
- **Verdict by source:** Young START — RotoBaller [S4], RotoWire (QB12) [S15], Yahoo/NFL.com/Fantasy Life waiver pieces [S20][S21][S22]. Herbert SIT — SI [S5], RotoBaller [S4], PFN [S11]. Herbert START (low-end) — CBS only, on the strength of BUF allowing the 6th-most QB points [S10].
- **Disagreements to weigh:** (a) CBS is alone on Herbert. (b) SI's "bottom-5 fantasy offenses Week 3" list includes Carolina [S23] and RotoWire calls CLE "a step up" [S15]; the Lake Erie wind concern is real for a 42.5 total [S19][S40]. Net: the weight of sources says Young over Herbert, but expect a lower ceiling than Weeks 1-2. I could not retrieve the FantasyPros QB ECR.

- **Kraft:** 98.3% snaps and 84.4% route rate in Wk2 (up from 47.8%) [S83][S84]; only 2-7 last week, 5-80 on the season [S85]. Jayden Reed OUT (neck) frees targets [S86][S89]. - **Read:** Consensus rank order is Kraft (TE3) over Warren (TE5) [S81], and Kraft's matchup/usage case is better documented; Warren's edge is floor-by-TD and that his slot stays open until Sunday. No source found explicitly compared the two head-to-head (the FantasySP comparison page was blocked).

### 3. RB2/FLEX: Montgomery vs Hubbard vs Hall (Brown is a locked RB1 at RB10 [S27])

- **Hall:** 21 touches and a 2:1 snap edge over Allen [S31][S32]; DET is soft through the air, which suits Hall's 5-target role [S34]; risk is a 6.5-point road dog script and Allen's goal-line work [S31][S40]. Start everywhere found [S34][S35][S31][S32].

- **Order by sources:** Hubbard ≈ Hall > Montgomery. Pickens (14 tgt, 66 yds, 0 TD, but a 52.5 total in Rio [S42][S57]) is the WR alternative for the flex; NBC lists him as a regression-candidate [S61].
