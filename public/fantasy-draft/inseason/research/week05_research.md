# Week 5 research (2026-10-05)

Two research agents ran on Monday 10/5, after Week 4's Sunday games; Monday night's game had not
yet been played. Every fact comes from search-result summaries, because fantasy sites and Reddit
are blocked for direct fetch. Claims were checked against 2026 team context, and stale
2022-2025 items were dropped.

## Your roster

- **Ja'Marr Chase - concussion.** He was hurt in the 2nd quarter vs JAX: Jarrian Jones pulled his
  facemask and he took a second hit on the way down. He was ruled out with 3 catches for 27
  yards, and must clear the 5-step protocol before Sunday @MIA.
  - No Monday update from Zac Taylor was found.
  - Agent estimate: about **40% to play** Week 5.
    - He was diagnosed during the game rather than evaluated and cleared.
    - Miami is 0-4.
    - The Week 6 bye gives Cincinnati a reason to rest him until Week 7.
  - Tee Higgins also hurt an adductor late in the game, after 11 catches for 157 yards.
  - Sources:
    [ESPN](https://www.espn.com/nfl/story/_/id/50101891/bengals-star-wr-jamarr-chase-suffers-concussion-vs-jaguars),
    [NBC](https://www.nbcsports.com/fantasy/football/player-news/2026-10-04/jamarr-chase-enters-concussion-protocol),
    [Cincy Jungle (Higgins)](https://www.cincyjungle.com/cincinnati-bengals-injuries/201262/tee-higgins-adductor-injury-recovery-timetable-jaguars-game).
- **Breece Hall - quad.** He missed Week 4 and had no practice last week. No Monday update from
  Aaron Glenn was found.
  - In Week 4 Braelon Allen took every Jets RB carry (14 for 59).
  - About **35%** for Week 5 vs CLE.
  - Source: [NBC](https://www.nbcsports.com/fantasy/football/player-news/2026-10-04/braelon-allen-takes-every-nyj-rush-in-week-4).
- **DeVonta Smith - hamstring.** Garafolo says he is "at very best" questionable for Week 5 in
  London. Jeff McLane expects him to miss multiple games.
  - About **20%** for Week 5; Week 6-7 is the realistic return.
  - Source: [NBC](https://www.nbcsports.com/fantasy/football/player-news/2026-10-04/devonta-smith-questionable-at-very-best-for-week-5).
- **David Montgomery - losing work.** In Week 4 he had 10 carries for 24 yards and no targets.
  Woody Marks has scored the goal-line TD two weeks running.
  - Nico Collins is back (7 catches, 118 yards, 2 TD) and the Houston line is struggling. RB3.
  - Source: [CBS](https://www.cbssports.com/fantasy/football/news/texans-david-montgomery-bottled-up-in-week-4-loss/).
- **No new injuries:** Goff (412 yards), Hubbard (20-122-2, bye Week 5), Chase Brown (11-of-11
  catching), Waddle (5-35-1, the foot is fine), Warren (5-41), Kraft (6-42-1). Pickens plays

- **Steelers D/ST vs IND:** a decent mid-tier start.

  - Vs SF in Week 5.
