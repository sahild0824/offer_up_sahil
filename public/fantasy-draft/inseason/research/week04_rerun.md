## WR and TE scan

WR/TE scan re-run with web news (24 of 25 WebSearch calls used; search worked). Usage numbers are carried over from the prior nflverse run. What the news changes, in order:

TIGHT ENDS
(1) Isaiah Likely is still the best TE for rest of season, but by a smaller margin. Jaxson Dart is out for the whole regular season (full meniscus repair), so Jameis Winston starts every week. Likely scored 8.3 and 3.3 PPR with Winston, against 27.8 with Dart. His role is secure: 84% of snaps in Week 3, 24-31% target share, Theo Johnson down to 17% of snaps, Daniel Bellinger now on TEN.
(2) Juwan Johnson goes up. NFL.com's Week 4 waiver column leads with him and calls him a top-10 TE start vs ATL (Monday). The Week 3 line was 8/8 for 53 yards and 2 short TDs, with a late lost fumble. Nobody big is coming back to take his targets: Olave has played all three games (10-13 targets a week); Taysom Hill is not on NO's 2026 roster; rookie WR Tyson is on IR with a roughly two-month hamstring timeline dated 8/18, so he's unlikely to return before about Week 6-7. Noah Fant's rotation (57% of snaps, also 2 TDs in Week 3) is still the cap.
(3) Kenyon Sadiq is NOT on your screenshot list, so check whether he's available. Columns (4for4, SI, DraftKings, The Big Lead) have him as the #1 TE and a top overall add. But his 7-105-1 week came with Mason Taylor (thumb, week-to-week; coach Glenn said it "won't be long") and Adonai Mitchell both inactive. With Taylor healthy in Week 1, Sadiq played only 42% of snaps. He has more upside than Kraft but a shakier role than Likely.

WIDE RECEIVERS
(4) Keenan Allen is still the best short-term WR add. Alec Pierce reinjured his surgically repaired left heel in Week 2 and went on IR 9/23: out at least 4 weeks, with the team saying he "hopes to return this year". Allen's role looks solid through at least Week 6, likely longer. NEW RISK: Allen faces a minimum 3-game NFL suspension for his 8/30 drunk-driving arrest. Reports say it could start in Week 8 at the earliest, if the NFL waits for the court case.
(5) Jordan Addison goes down. Coach O'Connell says Jefferson's ankle sprain is not long-term and he could play Week 4; only the swelling is in question. Addison is a WR2/3 only if Jefferson is ruled out.
(6) Kalif Raymond: Caleb Williams has a Grade 2 hamstring strain (reported 3-4 weeks). The Week 4 starter is Keenum or Tyson Bagent, and Ben Johnson won't commit. Raymond is CHI's leading receiver and beat Odunze in all three games (Odunze had 3-44 on 6 targets in Week 3). He's a reasonable swap for Odunze, not a priority claim. DJ Moore is on BUF in 2026, so he doesn't matter for CHI.
(7) Tyreek Hill: CONFIRMED still unsigned. His 9/28 news was a tweet ("almost time to get off the couch and get to work"). His agent Rosenhaus expects a signing with a contender around October. There is no reported visit or offer; there's speculation about SF, LAR and KC, but no reported contact with SF. Watch only.
(8) Malik Washington: Achane (torn ACL) is done for the season. Washington has the most targets on MIA (23) and ran a route on 83% of pass plays in Week 3, but the offense is weak (13.5 implied) and his bye is Week 6. PASS.
(9) The rest:
- Mitchell: finger not fractured, but splinted, with "some concern" (9/25). PASS until he practices.
- Boston: blanked until the 4th quarter in Week 3 while Watson fed TE Fannin (7 catches, 2 TDs). Stash only.
- Meyers: upgraded slightly. Brian Thomas Jr. fell to 23 snaps vs 50 for Meyers.
- Mack Hollins: A.J. Brown's high-ankle timeline is 6-8 weeks (first chance about Week 7-8, not Week 6), so Hollins' window is longer than the prior run said. Still depth.
- Shakir: Josh Allen's knee (helmet hit in Week 3) is expected to be fine. PASS.
- Pittman, Tucker, Tate: no new news; verdicts unchanged.
