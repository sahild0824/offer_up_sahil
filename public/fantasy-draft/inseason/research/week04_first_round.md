WEEK 4 ROSTER NEWS for 'Kai and his Guy' (as of Tue 9/29; official practice reports start Wed 9/30).

TOP PRIORITY, BREECE HALL: The Jets call it a thigh injury; ESPN and NFL Network (Rapoport) say right quad. He got hurt on a 2nd-and-goal run from the DET 4 on the opening drive of the 4th quarter of the 31-24 loss at Detroit. LB Trevor Nowaske tackled him, he went to the sideline medical tent and was ruled out, and afterward he said he "felt something." That is why his snap share fell to 48% (32 snaps) while Braelon Allen got 52% (34). Monday's MRI was better than feared and he is officially week-to-week. SI reports the early fear was 3-6 weeks and the best case is now 1-2 missed games. Aaron Glenn said Monday: "I'm concerned with Breece... I just don't know the extent," with no update until Wednesday. He has had no practices yet. Nearly every outlet expects him to miss Week 4 @CHI, and ESPN's 0.0 projection agrees, though nothing is official. Without Hall, Allen is the lead back (per ESPN's Rich Cimini). Isaiah Davis is the change-of-pace back and has played zero offensive snaps this season (special teams only). One outlet, via search summary, expects Allen to handle about 70% of the rushing. CBS projects Allen for 19 touches and 12.3 PPR points, a top-20 RB; ESPN has him at 12.8. The Jets also lost LG Dylan Parham (knee, air cast), which hurts the run game.
Verdict: claim Braelon Allen. For this roster he is the highest-leverage add. He is Hall's direct handcuff, and the next byes hit right where he helps: Week 5 takes Hubbard (CAR) and Butker (KC); Week 6 takes Goff, Chase Brown and Ja'Marr Chase. He is not a lock for rest of season because Hall's best case is back in 1-2 weeks, so bid moderately if FAAB. Drop Odunze or Kraft. Keep Hall on the bench; do not drop him.

LINEUP: ESPN currently has Hall at RB2. Move Hubbard from FLEX into RB2, then fill FLEX with Pickens, Montgomery or Allen. They are near-equal this week (engine 13.9/13.7; Allen 12.3-12.8). Montgomery has the softest matchup (DAL defense is decimated, DvP x1.16) but is losing work to Woody Marks. Pickens has target volume (11 targets in Week 3) but plays @HOU.

Everyone else is healthy, with no new Week 3 injuries found: Goff (strong: CAR is expected to be without both starting CBs), Chase Brown (his Week 3 dip was game script, not role; snap share steady at 70%), Ja'Marr Chase, DeVonta Smith (cleared his hamstring before Week 3 and played 100% of snaps), Warren (IND@WAS is the London game at Tottenham, 9:30 AM ET Sunday, so his slot locks early), Hubbard (workhorse with Brooks on IR, 84% snaps), Butker (KC @LV dome). Steelers D/ST @CLE Thursday is a fine start: CLE implied 18.0 and the Browns' center Elgton Jenkins is in the concussion protocol. Herbert (@SEA) and Kraft (@TB) are clear bench, and Odunze's calf Questionable tag is stale.
