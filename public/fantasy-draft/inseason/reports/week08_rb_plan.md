# Running-back trade plan for the rest of the season (Tuesday 10/27, before Week 8)

**How this was built**
- **Engine:** exact injury-scenario checks of 30+ RB trades. Gains are lineup points over
  Weeks 8-17.
- **Rosters:** from the Week 7 screenshots (`examples/league_week08.json`). Moves since Thursday
  10/22 are unknown.
- **News:** `examples/news_week08.json`.
- **Data:** nflverse through Week 7, and FantasyPros rest-of-season ranks from 10/23.
- **Research agent:** Week 7 RB news, Week 7 trade charts (no Week 8 charts were out yet), and
  RB playoff schedules for Weeks 15-17.
- "Adjusted" gains assume Hubbard loses about 15% once Jonathon Brooks returns (Weeks 9-11).

## Why trade for an RB now
- **Chase Brown:** 8.4 PPR a game over Weeks 5-7, at 3.5 yards per carry, with Perine taking
  goal-line work. The market still has him at RB10, so he is a **sell**.
- **Hubbard:** at peak value (CBS 33, more than Brown), but Brooks returns in Weeks 9-11. **Sell.**
- **Hall:** inactive four straight games; Braelon Allen is the Jets' lead back.
- **Monangai:** hoping to return in Week 8.

**Swift (Rowan's)**
- Averages 19.8 a game over Weeks 5-7 on 86% of snaps.
- His playoff schedule (BUF, GB, DET) is among the easiest of the targets.
- You also own Monangai, so you would hold the whole Bears backfield.
- Rowan's only QB is Dak, whose bye is Week 14, so Love covers it for them.

**Henry (Audubon)**
- Audubon lost Hurts for the season, has only Purdy, and SF is on bye in Week 8, so they need a QB.
- **Risk:** Henry is 32 and scored 5.0 and 5.9 the last two weeks on 51% of snaps. BAL is
  struggling, and his playoff schedule is a little tough.

**Bijan (Rowan's)**
- Bijan is RB2 overall. Lamb is your WR1, but trading him also removes the Week 14 problem where
  Lamb and Pickens are both on bye.
- After the trade your WRs would be Pickens, Smith and Waddle.
- Bijan's bye is Week 11 (ATL), the same week as Kraft and Love.
