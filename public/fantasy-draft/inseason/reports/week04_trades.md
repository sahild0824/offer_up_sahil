# Week 4 - trades to propose

Built 2026-09-29 with `trades.py`, which checks every 1-for-1, 2-for-1 and 2-for-2 swap with
all nine teams. The full list is in the finder output at the end.

A trade only survives if it passes three tests:
- **You:** it improves your season lineup over Weeks 5-17, simulated week by week with byes
  and this week's injury news.
- **Them:** it does not hurt their lineup.
- **Market:** it is fair on FantasyPros rest-of-season trade values, so it does not read as a
  lowball.

The gains below are measured against your roster **after** tonight's claims: Allen for Herbert,
and Odunze's spot used for streams, which in Week 6 is the QB.

**Rosters are approximate.** Other teams' rosters are their real-draft Rounds 1-11, minus anyone
now on your waiver wire. Their waiver adds since the draft are unknown, so check the two rosters
below before you send anything.

## Propose first: Amon-Ra St. Brown for Chase Brown + George Pickens (to Oh Saquon You See)

**What it does for you: +42 lineup points over Weeks 5-17, about +3.3 a week.**

- Amon-Ra is ranked 5th overall rest of season. The model has him at 19.9 a game, the most on
  either side of any trade here.
- Paired with Ja'Marr Chase, you have two WR1s and DeVonta Smith at FLEX.
- The spot this 2-for-1 opens gets the best wire add (Kalif Raymond or Juwan Johnson).

**Why they should say yes:**

- They lost De'Von Achane for the season. Chase Brown fills that hole directly: RB ranked 14th
  overall rest of season, with 100% of Cincinnati's goal-line work.
- Pickens, WR ranked 18th with targets rising 6-8-11, replaces much of the receiver production
  they give up.
- On trade charts they come out ahead: 82 received for 71 given. The model has their lineup
  +21.5.

**Check before you send:**

- **Amon-Ra's health.** In Week 3 he played only 48 of 65 snaps, left the field slowly several
  times, and scored 11.9. Dan Campbell: "He just had a little something... I don't think it's a
  huge thing." Wait for Wednesday's practice report. If he is limited or out, hold the offer.
- **Their roster.** If they already claimed Ollie Gordon II or another starting RB, their need
  is smaller, and the Olave version below becomes the better ask.
- **Week 6 bye.** Detroit's Week 6 bye lands on the same week as Goff and Chase, so that week
  gets thinner (Chase Brown is on bye then too, so the swap is neutral there). The model counts
  it.

**If they decline, counter with Chris Olave for Chuba Hubbard + George Pickens.**

- It is worth slightly more to you (+45), because you keep Chase Brown.
- For them: Hubbard is a starting RB (84% of snaps since Brooks went on IR). The model has them
  +18.
- It is slightly thinner for them on trade charts (55 received for 58 given), so it is the
  second offer, not the first.

## Propose second: Kyren Williams for David Montgomery + Tucker Kraft (to Hakuna Mailata)

**What it does for you: +22.5 over Weeks 5-17.**

- Two bench pieces become a starting RB. Kyren is 14th among RBs in points per game (15.5,
  15.7, 21.8).
- He has handled 6 of the Rams' 8 RB red-zone carries. The risk is Blake Corum, who matched him
  at 12 carries in Week 2.
- The spot this opens gets Juwan Johnson, which also covers Warren's Week 13 bye, the job Kraft
  was doing.

**Why they should say yes:**

- Their only drafted TE, Dallas Goedert, has an MCL sprain and is week-to-week. Kraft starts for
  them now.
- Montgomery backs up James Cook.
- It is dead even on trade charts (26 for 25). The model has their lineup +20.
- Goedert may return soon, so propose now while the hole is real.

## Both together: +78 over Weeks 5-17, about +6 a week

That is more than every waiver move this week combined.

## Looked at and not proposed

- **Jonathan Taylor or CeeDee Lamb from Hurts so Good.** The finder likes both (+40 or more),
  but every version sends away Tyler Warren. Juwan Johnson would then be your starting TE, and
  his role is sliding into a platoon.
- **Derrick Henry for Chase Brown + Hubbard (Audubon).** +47 on paper, but it leans on a
  32-year-old repeating a 23.6 points-a-game start. It also collides with the Amon-Ra offer,
  since both send Chase Brown.
- **Trey McBride for Chase Brown + Kraft (Soaring Wings).** +35, and they need an RB with
  Etienne out. It collides with both offers above. Keep it as the fallback if Oh Saquon and
  Hakuna both say no.
- **Ashton Jeanty from NW BIRDIES.** They are RB-rich and WR-poor, but every version the model
  tried lowers their lineup. They should not accept, and a rejected offer costs you nothing
  except information.
- **Buy-lows the model cannot see.** Puka Nacua and Nico Collins look cheap because missed
  games count as zeros. Both have injury questions (Nacua's groin, Collins' hamstring), so the
  model's caution is not wrong this week.
